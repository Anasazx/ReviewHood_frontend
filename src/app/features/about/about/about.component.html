About us

Built for honest reviews

ReviewHood is a place where Tunisians share real opinions on products they've actually bought — no fake ratings, no paid placements.

Why we built this

Too many review platforms are cluttered with fake ratings, paid placements, and reviews that read like ads. We wanted a simple place where people could find out what a product is actually like — from people who bought it, not from marketing.

How we keep it honest

No fake ratings

Every review comes from a real account. We don't buy, sell, or manufacture ratings.

No paid placements

Companies can't pay to rank higher, hide bad reviews, or boost their score.

Real Tunisian shoppers

Our community reviews products they've bought locally, so recommendations actually apply to you.
